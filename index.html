Know Us Better

About SKCT Events

Sri Krishna College of Technology's Event Management Team organizes India's premier inter-college symposium, bringing together talented students from across the country. Our mission is to provide a platform for innovation, creativity, and skill development.

Since our inception, we've hosted over 50 successful events with participation from 100+ colleges. We're committed to fostering excellence, providing career opportunities, and creating unforgettable experiences for every participant.

Terms & Conditions

🚫 No Refund Policy

All registration fees are non-refundable under any circumstances. Please ensure your availability before registering.

📜 General Terms

  • Valid college ID required for entry
  • Event pass must be shown at all times
  • Management reserves the right to change event schedules
  • Participants must follow college code of conduct
  • Photography/videography allowed for promotional purposes

⚖️ College Policy

  • Zero tolerance for misconduct or violation of rules
  • Participants responsible for personal belongings
  • Medical emergencies handled by on-campus medical team
  • Accommodation subject to availability
  • Outside food and beverages not permitted

🏆 Competition Rules

  • Judges' decision is final and binding
  • Plagiarism results in immediate disqualification
  • Team members must be from the same college (unless specified)
  • Late submissions will not be accepted
  • Prize distribution within 7 working days
